About Us
Born here.
Guide here.
Suwon is our home, not our backdrop

Like a Local was built on one simple idea — the best guide is someone who actually lives there. Not someone who memorised a script, but someone who knows which galbi place the locals actually go to, which gate of the fortress has the best sunrise angle, which market stall has been run by the same grandmother for thirty years.

We keep our groups small because real experience doesn't scale. Eight guests maximum. Always.
