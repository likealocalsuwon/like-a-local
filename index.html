Why Suwon?

Seoul has the crowds.
Suwon has the soul.

12.8 million people visit South Korea every year. Most stay in Seoul. Here's what they're missing.

🏯

UNESCO World Heritage

Hwaseong Fortress — a complete 18th-century royal defense system, fully intact, walkable, and 40 minutes from Seoul.

🍖

Suwon Galbi

The city that invented Korean BBQ short ribs. You haven't eaten galbi until you've had it in Suwon.

🎨

Haenggung-dong

A neighborhood of indie coffee shops, craft workshops, and murals — the creative soul of the city, loved by locals.

🧺

Living Traditional Markets

Five real, active traditional markets. Not tourist traps — places where local grandmas do their weekly shopping.

👘

Hanbok & Living History

Palace performances, guard-changing ceremonies, and archery experiences. History that's still alive, not just preserved.

🚋

1 Hour from Seoul

Direct express train from Seoul Station. No flights, no transfers, no stress. Just arrive and explore.
