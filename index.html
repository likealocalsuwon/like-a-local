Why Suwon?

Seoul has the crowds.
Suwon has the soul.

12.8 million visitors come to Korea each year. Most stay in Seoul.

01
UNESCO World Heritage

Hwaseong Fortress — a complete 18th-century royal defence system, fully intact, walkable, 40 minutes from Seoul.

02
Suwon Galbi

The city that invented Korean BBQ short ribs. You haven't eaten galbi until you've had it here, on Ingye-dong street.

03
Haenggung-dong

Indie cafés, craft studios, murals — the creative neighbourhood locals love on weekends, untouched by tour buses.

04
Living Traditional Markets

Five real, active traditional markets where local grandmothers do their weekly shopping. A living city, not a set.

05
Palace Performances

Guard-changing ceremonies, archery experiences, hanbok. History still alive, not just preserved behind glass.

06
1 Hour from Seoul

Direct express train from Seoul Station. No flights, no transfers. Step off and we take it from there.
