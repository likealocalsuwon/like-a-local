About Like a Local

Suwon is one hour from Seoul.
Yet most visitors never arrive.

Like a Local is an AI-powered travel matching platform that connects Western independent travelers with Suwon's authentic local experiences — from the walls of Hwaseong to the alleyways of Haenggung-dong. We pair each traveler's unique interests with the right guides and local businesses, creating journeys that no guidebook could script.
